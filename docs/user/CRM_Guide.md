# CRM: birthdays and holidays

Practice CRM is for client care, not marketing campaigns. There is no automated birthday email in this phase.

Path: **Practice → CRM** (`/crm`). Permission: `crm.view` (edit holidays: `crm.manage`).

## Birthdays

The desk lists clients with a **date of birth** in this month and next month.

1. Set DOB on the client KYC profile (`Clients_KYC_Guide.md`).
2. Open CRM. Count cards and the table link to the client profile.
3. Optional filter: responsible lawyer (from open matters).
4. Export the month list (PDF, Word, Excel) from the CRM page.

Greet clients in person or with a one-off email from the client profile. Do not bulk-mail from CRM.

## Public holidays

Zimbabwe public holidays for this year and next seed on first CRM visit. Managing Partners and secretaries can add or remove firm holidays.

Use holidays when planning hearings and leave. They do not auto-block the calendar.
