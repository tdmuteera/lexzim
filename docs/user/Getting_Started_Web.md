# Getting started (web)

LexZim is a **hosted web practice system** for Zimbabwe law firms. You use it in a browser. There is no desktop install for the pilot.

## What you need

- A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari)
- Your firm’s LexZim web address (HTTPS), for example a Railway URL such as `https://your-app.up.railway.app`
- The email and temporary password your Managing Partner or LexZim ops gave you
- A working internet connection (sign-in, AI, and sync all need the network)

## First login

1. Open your firm’s LexZim URL.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. If this is your first time, change the temporary password when asked (or ask your Managing Partner to reset it).
4. You should land on the **Dashboard**.

If login fails, check that you are on the correct firm URL and that Caps Lock is off. See **FAQ.md** for password reset and uptime notes.

## After you sign in (Managing Partner)

On the Dashboard you may see a **Getting started** checklist. It is guidance only. You can dismiss it anytime. It does **not** block the rest of the app.

Typical first steps:

1. **Settings → Firm profile**: confirm firm name, contact details, default currency (for example USD or ZWG), and invoice prefix.
2. **Settings → Licence**: confirm trial or paid edition and seat count.
3. **Lawyers & Staff**: invite colleagues within your seat limit.
4. **Settings → AI**: choose a provider if your firm will use the assistant. Web lookup stays **off** unless you turn it on.
5. Create a **client** and a **matter**.
6. **Settings → Backup & Export**: run an export when you are ready, or acknowledge that you will schedule one.

## Roles you will see

| Role | Typical use |
|------|-------------|
| Managing Partner | Firm settings, users, licence, export, full practice |
| Senior / Junior Associate | Matters, documents, calendar, AI (as permitted) |
| Secretary | Practice support within their permissions |
| Accounts | Billing and trust (as permitted) |

Menus hide items you are not allowed to open. That is normal.

## Safety defaults

- Your firm’s data is separated from other firms (tenant isolation).
- **Trust money** and **fee billing** are separate modules. Do not treat a fee receipt as a trust deposit.
- AI answers are assistance only. They are not a substitute for your professional judgment.
- Web lookup for AI is off by default.
