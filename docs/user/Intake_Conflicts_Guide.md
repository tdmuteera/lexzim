# Intake and conflicts guide

Use **Intake & Conflicts** for new prospects **before** you open a full client and matter. There is no public client portal in this phase. Staff enter prospects inside LexZim.

## Why this workflow exists

Zimbabwe practices need a clear conflict habit. Intake captures the prospect and opposing party text, runs a search, then allows conversion only when the gate passes (or an authorised override is recorded).

## Capture a prospect

1. Open **Intake & Conflicts**.
2. Create a new intake: prospect name, contact details, area of law, short matter summary, **opposing party**, assignee.
3. Save. This does **not** auto-create a client.

Statuses you will see include open (active), declined, archived, and converted.

## Run a conflict check

On the intake detail page, run the conflict search.

- The search looks across clients, related parties, matters, and other intakes in **your firm only**.
- Exact name matches are treated as conflict signals.
- Partial matches may show as possible conflicts for human review.

Record the outcome. Do not convert while a blocking conflict remains unresolved.

## Override (when allowed)

If policy allows, an authorised user may override with notes. Overrides are audited. Use them sparingly and write a clear reason.

## Convert to client and matter

When the conflict gate is clear (or a valid override exists):

1. Open the convert wizard on the intake.
2. Confirm client details (new or existing, as the wizard allows).
3. Confirm matter title and basics.
4. Complete conversion.

The intake becomes **converted** and links to the new or selected client and matter.

If the firm **licence is expired**, conversion that would create a new matter may be blocked. Export still works. Ask your Managing Partner to renew.

## Decline or archive

- **Decline** when the firm will not take the work.
- **Archive** when the prospect is closed without conversion.

## Good habits

- Always fill opposing party when known.
- Search again if names change before conversion.
- Prefer intake for walk-ins and phone enquiries instead of opening a matter first.
- Keep conflict notes factual and short.
