# Daily lawyer guide

Practical day-to-day use for associates and partners working files in LexZim.

## Start of day

1. Open **Dashboard** for overdue procedures, hearings, and reminders (scoped to your matters unless you can view firm-wide).
2. Open **Reminders** for a focused list of what is due soon.
3. Open **Calendar** for hearings and appointments.

## Clients

**Clients** holds individual and company records.

- Search before creating a duplicate.
- Capture contact details and identification notes as your firm requires.
- Link work to clients through **matters**, not loose notes only.

## Matters

**Matters** is the centre of the file.

- Open or create a matter under the right client.
- Set area of law, status, responsible lawyer, court and case number when known.
- Use the matter timeline, procedures, documents, time, and costs panels as your firm practices.

Statuses typically move from prospective or open through on hold to closed or archived. Follow your firm’s file-opening rules.

## Documents and templates

**Documents** stores matter files. Templates can generate Word drafts with merge fields.

- Upload to the correct matter.
- Generated drafts are starting points. Always review before filing or sending.

## Calendar and deadlines

- Diary hearings and appointments on **Calendar**.
- Track filing deadlines and matter procedures so the Dashboard and Reminders stay useful.
- Browser notifications (if enabled by the firm) only work while LexZim is open in the browser. There is no separate mobile push in the MVP.

## Time and costs on a matter

On a matter you can record:

- **Time entries** (with rate when billable)
- **Disbursements / costs** on the fee side

Accounts or partners later pull unbilled time and costs into a fee note under **Billing**. That is separate from trust.

## AI on a matter

If you have AI permission:

- Use **AI Assistant** for grounded research questions.
- Use matter assist tools only when your firm allows matter context.
- Treat every answer as a draft for your review. Cite and verify against primary sources before relying on it.

## Collaboration

- Responsible lawyer and matter team control who sees a matter when you do not have firm-wide view.
- Do not paste confidential client material into tools outside LexZim unless your firm policy allows it.

## End of day

- Update procedure status and next hearing dates.
- Save important documents to the matter.
- Check Reminders once more before you leave.
