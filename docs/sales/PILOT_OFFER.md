# LexZim pilot offer

Structured offer for evaluation firms (for example a first commercial pilot such as T Pfigu Attorneys). Adjust bracketed fields per deal. Do not invent testimonials.

## Pilot goal

Prove that the firm can run daily practice on hosted LexZim: matters, diary, billing, trust, and optional AI, with a clear exit path via export.

## Success criteria (agree in writing)

Example checklist (edit per firm):

1. Managing Partner and at least one other role signed in successfully
2. Firm profile and currency set
3. At least one real or anonymised matter opened end to end (documents or time as relevant)
4. Fee note **or** trust entry demonstrated without mixing the two
5. One firm export downloaded and stored securely by the firm
6. AI used once with a verified, non-filed research question (optional if AI declined)

## Honesty commitments (LexZim)

- Hosted product; no claim of permanent offline practice mode
- AI needs internet; Zimbabwe grounding is assistance, not a substitute for counsel
- Trust module supports good practice but does not replace bank reconciliation or professional duties
- We do not hold data hostage: export works if the licence later expires
- We do not publish fake client quotes

## Kickoff agenda (60 to 90 minutes)

1. Access URL, logins, roles
2. Firm profile and licence screen
3. Create client / matter path
4. Calendar and reminders
5. Billing vs trust (five-minute contrast)
6. AI settings and safety defaults (web lookup off)
7. Export and Help guides
8. Agree success criteria and support contact

## End of pilot

- Review success criteria
- Decision: convert to Solo/Firm subscription, extend pilot, or exit
- If exiting: confirm latest export is in the firm’s custody
- If converting: issue licence (`docs/LICENSING.md`) and confirm seats
