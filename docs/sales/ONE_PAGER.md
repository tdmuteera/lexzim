# LexZim one-pager

**Practice software for Zimbabwe law firms. Hosted. Matter-centric. Trust-aware. AI-assisted.**

## The offer

LexZim helps small and mid-size Zimbabwe practices run files in one shared web workspace: clients and matters, documents, diary and deadlines, intake with conflict checks, fee notes, and trust accounting. An optional AI assistant is grounded for Zimbabwe work and needs internet access.

## Who it is for

- Managing partners who want one system for the firm, not scattered spreadsheets
- Associates who live in matters, documents, and court dates
- Accounts staff who must keep **fees** and **trust** apart

## How it is delivered

- **Hosted web app** (browser + HTTPS URL). No desktop installer for the pilot era.
- Your firm is a **tenant**: data is isolated from other firms.
- Sign-in is email and password for firm users.
- Editions: **Trial**, **Solo**, **Firm** (seats and features). Prices: see `PRICING.md` ([TBD] where not set).

## Clear limits (say these upfront)

- Needs reliable internet for normal use and for AI
- AI is assistance only. Lawyers verify before filing or advising
- Export is available; full restore into a new environment is support-assisted in MVP
- This is not a return to “local-only PC databases.” Control and exit are export plus tenant isolation

## Why firms switch

- One place for the file instead of email folders alone
- Conflict habit built into intake
- Trust and fees cannot be casually mixed in the product design
- Licence expiry does not lock your export
